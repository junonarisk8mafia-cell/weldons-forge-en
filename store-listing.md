## Full Description (max 4000 chars, currently ~2504 chars)

⚔️ MASTER JAPANESE WELDING — FROM SAFETY CERTS TO JIS CERTIFICATION

WELDON'S FORGE is a battle-style quiz game for foreign welders working
in Japan (or preparing to come). Fight through 6 stages and level up
from Day-1 legal permits to JIS certification and career strategy.

Made by a real Japanese shipyard welder — not a textbook company.
Every question reflects what actually happens on Japanese job sites.

🛡️ WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

STAGE 1 — Legal Permits & Safety
The certificates you legally need from Day 1 (Arc Welding Safety
Training, Grinding Wheel, Tamakake rigging, Full Harness), real
costs and course lengths in Japan, confined space rules, and the
PPE that Japanese sites actually inspect every morning.

STAGE 2 — Tools & Torch Parts
Guraindaa, chippaa, nozuru, rainaa — the tools and torch parts your
Japanese seniors will name out loud, and how to respond correctly.

STAGE 3 — Japanese Workplace Slang
Kachiage. Noro tore. Hatsuri shiro. Real slang you will hear on
your first week, with romaji pronunciation and what to actually DO
when you hear it. This stage alone can save you from serious
miscommunication.

STAGE 4 — Positions & Joint Types
JIS welding positions and symbols, fillet dimensions (kyakucho /
nodo-atsu), and the JIS-vs-AWS drawing traps that cause real rework.

STAGE 5 — Defects & NDT Inspection
Undercut, blowholes, lack of fusion, cold cracks — plus how VT, PT,
MT, RT and UT inspections work in Japanese quality culture.

STAGE 6 — Certifications & Career Path
JIS test codes (N-2F to N-2P), AW Kentei, Boiler Welder, WES, and a
realistic income roadmap for a welding career in Japan.

🇯🇵 EVERY QUESTION INCLUDES
• Japanese terms with kanji + romaji pronunciation
• A workplace TIP from real Japanese job-site experience
• Clear English explanations written for non-native speakers

🎮 GAME FEATURES
• 125+ questions across 6 stages
• XP and level-up system — unlock harder stages as you grow
• Review your answer history and retry weak areas
• Works offline — study anywhere: dorm, train, break room

👷 WHO THIS IS FOR
• Technical intern trainees (TITP / Ginou Jisshu) in welding
• Filipino, Vietnamese, Indonesian and other foreign welders in Japan
• Anyone preparing for a welding job in Japan
• Welders preparing for JIS qualification tests

Built with respect for the craft. Gambatte, welder. 🔥
